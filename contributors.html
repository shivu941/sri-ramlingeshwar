Teams Behind the Temple

The volunteer teams and committees who keep Sri Ramlingeshwar Prassanna running and growing, festival after festival.

Active Team

🎉 Festival & Utsav Committee

Supported by: Village Youth Association

Responsible for planning and running Sri Rama Navami, Deepavali, Hanuman Jayanti and the annual Pallaki Utsavam — including coordinating the palanquin procession from the village temple to the forest temple. Add real member names, the team lead's contact, and how new volunteers can join.

Active Team

🍚 Annadanam Team

Supported by: Local Families & Donors

Organises the community meal served on festival days and during special poojas, from sourcing ingredients to serving devotees. Add details on how families can sponsor an annadanam day.

Active Team

🧹 Maintenance & Upkeep Team

Supported by: Temple Trust

Handles daily cleaning, minor repairs, and general upkeep across both the village and forest temple premises. Add details on the team lead and how the trust funds this work.

Suggested Addition

🎶 Bhajan & Cultural Team

Not yet formed — recommended

A dedicated group to lead evening bhajans, organise cultural programs, and coordinate music during festivals and the Pallaki Utsavam procession. Many temples find this boosts festival attendance and gives younger devotees a way to get involved.

Suggested Addition

🧾 Donor & Sponsorship Coordination

Not yet formed — recommended

A small, transparent team to track pooja sponsorships and donations, and issue proper receipts once the temple's formal donation/UPI setup is ready. This builds donor trust and makes it easier to recognise contributors publicly.

Suggested Addition

🚸 Youth Seva & Volunteer Team

Not yet formed — recommended

Younger volunteers who help with crowd management, guest support, parking and setup during large festival gatherings — especially useful during the Pallaki Utsavam procession between the two temples.
