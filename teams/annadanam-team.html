Annadanam Team

📅 Jun 2022 – Present
🤝 Supported by: Local Families & Donors

The Annadanam Team organises the community meal served on festival days and during special poojas — from sourcing ingredients and cooking to serving devotees who visit the temple.

Meals are made possible through contributions from local families and donors, often sponsoring a specific festival day in memory of a loved one or to mark an occasion.
