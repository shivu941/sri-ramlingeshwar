Festival & Utsav Committee

📅 Jan 2023 – Present
🤝 Supported by: Village Youth Association

The Festival & Utsav Committee plans and runs the temple's major celebrations across the year — Sri Rama Navami, Deepavali, Hanuman Jayanti, and the Temple Anniversary.

The committee also coordinates the annual Pallaki Utsavam, organising the palanquin procession from the village temple to the forest temple, including routing, volunteers, and safety arrangements along the way.
