ONE DEITY, TWO SHRINES

The Village Temple & the Forest Temple

Sri Ramlingeshwar is present both in the village and in the forest — the two shrines are deeply connected. During major festivals, the deity's pallaki (palanquin) is carried in procession from the village temple to the forest temple, retracing a tradition followed for generations.
